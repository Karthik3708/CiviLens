Who is eligible to apply for National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship
Scheme (NMMSS)?

Students belonging to any community’ studying in Government,
Government-aided or local body schools and fulfilling the Scheme guidelines
are eligible to apply for the scholarship. Students whose parental income
from all sources is not more than Rs. 3,50,000/- per annum are eligible to
avail the scholarship. The students should score minimum of 55 % marks or
equivalent grade in Class VII examination for appearing in selection test for
award of scholarship (relaxable by 5% for SC/ST students).

What is the amount of scholarship under NMMSS?
The amount of scholarship is 12000 per annum.

What is the general procedure for the selection of awardee
students?

Each State/UT will conduct its own test at stage of class VIII for selection of
students for the award of the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship. The
state Level Examination may consist of the following two tests:

i. Mental Ability Test (MAT)
ii. Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT)

The students should score minimum of 55 % marks or equivalent grade in
class VII examination for appearing in selection test conducted during class
VIII for award of scholarship (relaxable by 50% for SC/ST students). The
student should be studying as regular student in Government, Government-
aided and local body schools.

Duration of the Test

The selection test for the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme
(NMMSS) is divided into two papers, and duration of 90 minutes is set for
each one. The total duration of the entire examination is 3 hours (180

What is the criteria for selection of students?

The students must pass both the tests i.e. MAT and SAT with at least 40%
marks in aggregate for these two tests. For SC/ST students, this cut off will
be 32% marks.

At the time of selection for writing the NMMSS Examination, the student
must have scored at least 55% marks or equivalent grade in Class VIII
examination. There will be 5% relaxation in marks for SC/ST students.

Eligibility and other conditions for commencement and continuation of
Scholarship at Secondary and Higher Secondary Stage: -

The awardees should get clear promotion from Class VIII to Class IX for
being eligible for commencement of the scholarship as indicated above;

The scholarship at the Secondary and Higher Secondary stage or equivalent
classes IX to XII is payable for a maximum period of four years for studies in
India only;

No Scholarship at this stage is payable for studying diploma/certificate level
courses;

For continuing the scholarship in class X and XII, the awardees should get
clear promotion from class IX to Class X and from class XI to Class XII in the
first attempt;

The awardees must obtain a minimum of 60% marks in Class X examination
(relaxable by 5% for SC/ST students) or equivalent examination for
continuation of the scholarship at higher secondary stage.

How can I apply online for scholarship?

National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) is on-boarded on
NSP. You can apply for fresh or renewal Scholarship under the Scheme on
the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at www.scholarships.gov.in.

How to apply for scholarship?

First, student has to register on National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at
www.scholarships.gov.in in order to get OTR ID. Next, the OTR must be
used to login on NSP Application Form module. Student's demographic
details and photograph will be automatically fetched from OTR.

What is One-Time Registration (OTR)?

One-Time Registration (OTR) is a unique number provided to streamline the
registration process. Users register with their mobile number and complete
eKYC (electronic Know Your Customer). Once this process is complete, an
OTR number is generated and sent to the user's phone. This unique number
allows users to log in and access various services without needing to re-
register. The OTR number is generated once and remains valid throughout
the student's academic career.

How to update demographic details?

For change in demographic details student must first update their Aadhaar
details. These changes will be reflected in student's OTR profile through
fresh _eKYC. After updation in NSP OTR, details will be reflected in NSP
Application Form.

What are the benefits of One-Time Registration?
One-Time Registration (OTR) offers several advantages:

e It helps in streamlining the registration process as OTR simplifies the
registration process by providing users with a unique number after
completing e-KYC. This eliminates the need for repetitive registration steps
across different services.

e It is convenient for the users as they only need to register once with their
mobile number and complete e-KYC to receive their OTR number. This
reduces the hassle of filling out multiple registration forms for various
services.

e The OTR number is generated once and remains valid throughout the
student's academic career, providing a consistent and stable identifier for all
related services.

e By eliminating the need for repetitive registrations, OTR enhances the
overall user experience, making it more convenient for traceability and
tracking to access multiple services.

Which portals typically use One-Time Registration?

The NSP portal and other state portals may implement One-Time
Registration (OTR) to simplify the registration process and ensure de-
duplication.

Is One-Time Registration mandatory for all services on NSP?

Yes, One-Time Registration is mandatory for all services on NSP. It serves as
the username for logging in and is also required to complete the face
authentication process.

Can I update my information after completing the One-Time
Registration (OTR) process?

Yes, you can update most of your information, including Aadhaar-related
details and other personal information. This ensures that your profile
remains accurate and up to date. If your Aadhaar information changes, you
will need to perform eKYC and face authentication again to update your
details.

How frequently should I update my One-Time Registration
information?

It is advisable to review and update your OTR information periodically,
especially if there are changes in your personal details or contact
information. Keeping your profile up to date ensures accuracy and enhances
the effectiveness of the OTR system.

What is a Reference no?

A Reference no is assigned when users provide their own Aadhaar or their
guardian's Aadhaar. It is sent to the registered mobile number to complete
face authentication and to generate OTR number.

What is Aadhaar based Payment?

Applicants/Students who have entered Aadhaar in their online application
correctly and if their Aadhaar is linked to any of their bank account, in such
cases the amount of Scholarship will be credited to that Aadhaar linked bank
account only even if the student has mentioned any other non-Aadhaar
linked bank account in her/his online application.

Which fields can I edit before final submission of application?

You can edit all details except “Registration Details”. It may be noted that
once you ‘finally submit’ your application it will be forwarded to the next
level and after that you cannot edit further.

Which fields in the application form are mandatory?

Fields provided with red asterisk (*) mark are mandatory fields.

Do I have to fill up the online application in one sitting?

No. You can fill up the online application in many sittings using ‘Save Draft’,
until you are satisfied that you have entered all desirable fields correctly.
The software provides facility to save your application at every stage until
you click on ‘Submit’ button.

Is there any Application ID?

Yes. An Application ID will be provided to the candidate once his/her
application is submitted online. It will be conveyed to candidates through
SMS. Students should remember their ID as it will be required while applying
for renewal during subsequent years. Receipt of ID does not give in itself a
guarantee of award of scholarship.

What should I do, if my Institute name is not appearing in the
dropdown list of Institutes in application form?

You may ask your institute to contact the concerned State Nodal officer of
NMMSS for their registration through U-DISE code available on NSP portal.

What should I do, if I do not find my Course name in the dropdown
menu?

You should immediately approach the institute (where you are studying) to
login their account and add the concerned Course from the Master course
list. Thereafter, you may be able to find the desired course name in your
online application.

What should I do before making any call/enquiry/representation at
helpdesk of National Informatics Centre (NIC) and/or Ministry of
Education?

You should first check your online status on NSP and then make any
call/enquiry/representation at helpdesk of National Informatics Centre (NIC)
and/or Ministry of Education (MoE).

What is the process of verification of my application?

Your application must be verified at School/Institute level and then at
domicile district level. You may contact the concerned verification authorities

What should I do for the successful payment of Scholarship in my
bank account?

You are advised to keep your bank account active to receive the
scholarship amount (till the disbursal of scholarships). Confirm from your

transaction failure/return of scholarship amount.
