Ministry of Labour & Employment

Financial Assistance for Education to the Wards of
Beedi/Cine/IOMC/LSDM Workers — Pre & Post-Matric

> Introduction:-

Financial Assistance for Education is provided to the wards of Beedi/Cine/Iron Ore,
Manganese Ore & Chrome Ore Mine (IOMC) Workers and Limestone & Dolomite Mine
(LSDM) and Mica Workers, currently ranging from Rs. 1,000/- to Rs. 25,000/- per student
per annum. The benefits under the scheme are being transferred through Direct Benefit
Transfer (DBT) system and the applications under this scheme are invited in online mode only

through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) — Attps://scholarships.gov.in.
The following categories of workers are eligible under this Scheme.
I. Financial Assistance for Education (Scholarship) to the Children of Beedi Workers.

II. Financial Assistance for Education (Scholarship) to the Children of Iron Ore, Manganese
Ore & Chrome Ore Mine (IOMC) Workers.

III. Financial Assistance for Education (Scholarship) to the Children of Limestone & Dolomite
Mine (LSDM) and Mica Workers.

IV. Financial Assistance for Education (Scholarship) to the Children of Cine Workers.

(i). National Scholarships Portal (NSP) (www.scholarships.gov.in) shall provide the timelines
for opening & closing of the portal.

(ji). The Guidelines for registration, application submission, verification, and disbursement of
Financial Assistance (scholarship) will be as per the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) regulations.

promoted to the next class are also eligible. The scholar must have taken regular admission in a

II. लौह अयस्क, मैंगनीज अयस्क एवं क्रोम अयस्क खान (आईओएमसी) कामगारों के बच्चों को शिक्षा के लिए वित्तीय
